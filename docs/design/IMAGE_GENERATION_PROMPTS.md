# 01-mobile-design-system.png

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create an original, premium, high fidelity MOBILE SOCIAL-COMMERCE APP design presentation. The source product is one mobile app with separate customer and merchant modes: content-led shoppable photos, multi-product hotspots, vertical reels, stories, creator storefronts, shopping, AI text/voice/telephone support, rewards, live commerce, and merchant operations. This is a design image, no application implementation.
Visual language for every board: warm ivory canvas #F5F5EF, white app surfaces #FFFFFF, almost-black green text #152B26, deep teal primary #126C55, chartreuse accent #D9EF72 used sparingly, cool grey separators #E0E6E1; muted coral and amber only for semantic states. Modern humanist sans typography with an Inter-like UI, clear large headings, tabular price numerals. A calm, editorial, highly polished commerce identity; generous 8px rhythm, 16px app gutters, rounded 16px cards, 12px fields, restrained soft shadows, 1.5px outline icon system. Filled teal primary buttons with white text; thin outlined secondary buttons; readable labels and prices; touch controls visually generous. No purple gradients, no excessive glassmorphism, no decorative 3D illustrations. Natural high quality product and creator photography: clothing, sneakers, bags, skincare, lamps, ceramics, headphones, home decor. Diverse authentic adults. Customer navigation: Home / Explore / Reels / Shop / Profile, with contextual cart, messages, notifications at top. Merchant navigation: Overview / Products / Create / Orders / More, with a visible Merchant mode badge and customer-mode switch.
Frame screens straight-on as flat UI artboards, consistent portrait aspect ratio near 9:19.5, subtle thin borders, no bulky device mockups, no angled phones, no overlapping artboards. Make every screen distinct, with logical workflows and convincing real interface content. Use short natural English copy, legible navigation and screen titles; sample prices in dollars are illustrative. Keep bottom safe areas and top status bars consistent.
Trust UX: distinguish social comments from verified purchase reviews; show Sponsored and Affiliate labels where applicable; call support explicitly AI; consequential AI actions show a confirmation before execution; order and payment success are distinct verified-result mockup states, not claims about a live system. Do not add desktop/admin screens because the user's requested artifact is mobile.

Asset type: master mobile design-system presentation, single very high resolution landscape image, ideally 3840 by 2560 or larger if supported.
Title, verbatim: "SOCIAL COMMERCE" and subtitle "Mobile design system & screen atlas".
Composition: sophisticated clean design studio board with a small left header and generous outer margins. Top 40% is a reusable UI kit in a precise modular grid. Bottom 60% features EIGHT large, fully detailed straight-on app screens in one orderly horizontal row, with small numbered labels beneath. No clipped content.
Top UI kit must visibly include:
1) Brand color swatches with exact hex labels #126C55 #D9EF72 #152B26 #F5F5EF #FFFFFF plus success/warning/error.
2) Typography samples "Aa", heading 32/40, title 24/32, body 16/24, label 12/16, numeric price "$84.00".
3) Primary, secondary, text, disabled, loading buttons; text input, search input, invalid field.
4) A product card with image/price/cart, a merchant card and Verified badge, avatars/story rings, shoppable product hotspot and compact product bottom sheet.
5) Customer bottom navigation and separate Merchant bottom navigation; segmented tabs and filter chips.
6) Status pills, toast, notification, confirmation dialog, skeleton loading, empty/error/offline mini states, simple chart and tiny table.
7) Spacing 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32, radius 12 / 16 / 24, 48px target, light/dark surfaces, reduced-motion note.
Eight hero screens bottom, numbered and clearly labeled:
01 "Home feed": story avatars, Following/For you selector, big lifestyle product post, two product hotspots, verified merchant, social actions and mini add-to-bag card; not a catalogue.
02 "Explore": visual masonry inspiration, category chips, search, visual-search camera.
03 "Reels": full bleed fashion demonstration video with subtitles, social rail and pinned product mini card.
04 "Product": large sneaker imagery, price, stars, variant chips, merchant, delivery and Add to cart.
05 "Live shopping": live creator video, viewers badge, live comments, pinned purchasable product and countdown.
06 "AI shopping": explicitly AI chat, budget question, two product recommendation cards and voice input.
07 "Checkout": separate shipments grouped by merchant, address, delivery, payment methods card/mobile money/wallet and transparent order total.
08 "Merchant overview": Merchant mode badge, small sales chart, orders to fulfil, low stock, content performance and dedicated merchant tabs.
Show the system visually rather than long paragraphs. A final thin footer lists the atlas sections "Access • Social discovery • Shopping & orders • Rewards & creator commerce • Conversations & AI • Merchant mode". Clean editorial alignment, rich imagery, professional Figma-style design-system quality.
