# 2. UNDERSTAND THE PRODUCT VISION — MOST IMPORTANT

We are NOT building:

- A traditional e-commerce application.
- A normal online shopping website.
- A conventional product catalogue application.
- An Instagram clone with shopping buttons added.
- A generic marketplace with ordinary product cards.
- A traditional shopping application with a social media section.

We are building an entirely integrated, social-media-style commerce experience.

The platform should visually and behaviorally feel like a modern social networking application, but its central purpose is product discovery, product engagement, merchant relationships, and shopping.

### The central design concept

Imagine opening a social media platform where:

- Ordinary photo posts are replaced by engaging product posts.
- Ordinary video posts become shoppable product videos.
- Reels become immersive product discovery and shopping experiences.
- Stories become temporary product showcases, promotions, and offers.
- Profiles become interactive merchant storefronts and customer identities.
- Followers become customer–merchant relationships.
- Comments become product discussions and questions.
- Likes and reactions become product engagement.
- Shares and reposts become product recommendations.
- Saved posts become shopping wishlists and product collections.
- Direct messages become conversational shopping experiences.
- Live streams become interactive live-shopping events.
- Creator content becomes product promotion and affiliate commerce.
- Shopping becomes naturally integrated into every relevant social interaction.

Every important social interaction should have a meaningful commerce experience.

A customer should be able to discover a product through a feed, watch a demonstration, read discussions, ask questions, view customer experiences, inspect product information, and purchase it without feeling that they have left the social platform.

**The experience must feel like social media designed specifically for shopping, rather than e-commerce designed to look like social media.**

This distinction must guide every design decision.

### Important design principle

Do not make every screen look like a shopping catalogue.

For example:

The home screen should feel like a social feed, not a grid of ordinary product listings.

The reels screen should feel like an immersive video discovery platform, not a product listing page with videos.

Merchant profiles should feel like social profiles combined with interactive storefronts.

Messaging should feel like a modern social messaging application with native commerce capabilities.

The shop section can use conventional e-commerce discovery patterns where they improve usability, but it must remain visually consistent with the overall social-commerce identity.

Checkout should prioritize clarity, speed, trust, and simplicity rather than trying to look like a social feed.

Different screens should have their own appropriate interaction patterns while maintaining one cohesive design system.
